# Design and preview image references

The September 2026 Noor Daycare redesign uses Fredoka for display text and DM Sans for body copy, with red, blue, yellow, pink, and green accents. The homepage contains a hero followed by Why Families Choose Noor, Care For Each Stage, A Day At Noor, a condensed Schedule a Visit section, and a separate Parent Questions section. Per the latest owner request, the visit walkthrough and all public CAPS, tuition, and openings copy have been removed; FAQs use short answers about ages, hours, licensing, meals, and potty training. Age cards and a five-part daily timeline remain visible without switching tabs. Native question disclosures, mobile navigation, and a mobile booking dock support parent browsing. The booking dock stays available after the hero until the inline booking panel is in view.

The homepage uses the existing playroom and outdoor preview images, both explicitly labeled as not the actual daycare. The earlier photo-stack component and remaining image assets are retained in the project but are not rendered on the homepage.

The street address remains unpublished. The existing phone reveal button and click-to-call behavior are preserved. Hours (7:30 AM-6:00 PM) and ages (8 weeks through 4 years) were supplied by the owner. The 14 daily activities are transcribed from the owner's schedule photo, IMG_4301.HEIC, in their original order and grouped into five parts of the day. The photo does not specify individual activity times, so no exact intermediate times are assigned. The owner supplied the current capacity of 6 children, Bright from the Start licensing, CDA/CPR/First Aid credentials, potty training support, slides/swings/play area, affordability, and no CAPS acceptance for this redesign. Capacity is not represented as available openings.

Scheduling opens the owner's Day Care Tour event directly at https://calendly.com/noor-day-care/30min, through the official inline widget and booking links. Calendly handles booking information; the site does not store it. The provider name remains subtle, only in the licensing FAQ; Noor Daycare is the site and business display name.

Remaining content to supply: owner-approved real daycare photos, exact tuition and fees, current age-specific openings, and any holiday/closure details. Meals and snacks are described as part of the existing routine; no included-in-tuition, allergy-free, or special-diet guarantees are made.
